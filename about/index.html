About NIFA

New Ideas, Future Applications.

NIFA is a parent brand building purposeful products across different industries, needs and possibilities. Technology, with intention.

The brand idea

What NIFA means.

New Ideas, Future Applications. The name describes a method: begin with an idea worth testing, build toward an application that will still matter, and be honest about how far along it is.

“With intention” means each product exists to solve one clear problem, and what does not serve that problem is cut.

NIFA is a product ecosystem and parent brand, not a claim of scale. Its products are in development and built in the open on GitHub by Faizan Patel, with collaborators on some projects such as GramCare AI.

Not one thing, on purpose. NIFA is not only a wellbeing company, a game studio, a healthcare company or an AI agency. It is deliberately diverse, and it is not another name for Niyyah.

Business approach

How NIFA thinks about building, audiences and revenue.

This is the portfolio-level logic. Every product page then explains its own.

Problem first

A product starts with a specific friction in someone’s life or work, then uses the smallest technology that addresses it.

Independent bets

Four products in four markets spread risk. One product’s failure does not have to stop the others, and none depends on another’s backend.

Validate before monetising

Release, observe and learn first. Revenue ideas are written as proposals until real use supports them.

Trust as a commercial constraint

Prayer, notifications and health data are sensitive. Practices that erode trust, like intrusive ads or collecting notification content, are ruled out even if they would earn money.

Small and maintainable

A small team can only keep what it can maintain. Simple, explainable systems are preferred to impressive ones.

Honest status

Every claim is marked as documented, in progress, planned or proposed. No users, revenue, partnerships, validation or releases are invented.

Consumer products

Niyyah, Wrong Turn and QuietDude lean toward free cores with optional extras, because reach and trust come first.

Institutional product

GramCare AI would earn, if ever, through institutional licensing, services and funding, which need evidence and long sales cycles.

Portfolio effect

Shared design language and principles reduce duplicated effort, but each product must justify itself on its own market.

Principles

Shared standards.

  • Purpose over complexity.
  • Useful over novel.
  • Privacy-conscious engineering where the product allows.
  • Honest communication about what works, what is planned and what is not possible.
  • Separate identities, one standard.
  • Accessible and maintainable.
