Live with intention.

Niyyah is a calm Muslim companion. It gently locks your most distracting apps during prayer, then opens them again once you've prayed.

See how it works For investors

Your apps rest while you pray.

Choose the apps that pull your attention away. Niyyah locks them as a prayer window opens and lifts the lock the moment you confirm you've prayed.

  • Per-prayer settings, including Jumu'ah overrides.
  • A five-minute buffer before each prayer, so the lock is already in place.
  • Instant release. Verifying the prayer ends that lock at once, with no waiting for a countdown.
  • Pause and emergency override for travel, illness or real need. The override is deliberate and rate-limited, so the lock never traps you.

Confirm it in your own words.

After you pray, you confirm by voice or by typing. Speech recognition is tolerant of reasonable mis-transcriptions, so a small slip never locks you out.

Try it with your real microphone. Your browser asks for permission, then the demo scores what it heard against what you meant. Works best in Chrome or Edge, which send speech to their own recognition service.

    A month, reflected back to you.

    Each month, Niyyah gathers your prayers into a private Monthly Reflection: a rank and score, prayers completed out of 150, completion, your best streak, Fajr days, how many prayers you verified, and which prayers were missed.

    It is generated on your device and never becomes part of cloud sync.
